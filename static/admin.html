Assumptions behind "bins needed"

Bins needed per zone = zone waste (kg/day) × days between collections ÷ (bin litres × density × fill factor).
Zone waste = campus waste × zone's share of foot traffic.
Hotspot score components (each 0–1): reports = decayed weighted reports (no-bin 3, overflow 2, damaged 1) / (signal + 6); deficit = missing bins ÷ bins needed; traffic = zone ÷ busiest zone; overflow = overflow reports (14 d) per bin.
